# Scientific and editorial quality policy

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Peer-reviewed primary studies, methods, empirical results, and relevant foundational sources.
2. Primary preprints and technical reports, clearly marked **not peer reviewed** where applicable.
3. Official framework/vendor specifications and documentation, always with version and access date.
4. Independent replications and high-quality external analyses with methods.
5. Blogs, marketing, social posts and generated summaries only as leads or examples, **not independent proof**.

Source status matters: peer review alone does not guarantee correctness. Compare competing results and report study limitations. PRISMA 2020 may inform transparent reporting for a defined systematic-review subsection, but do **not** claim the entire textbook is a systematic review without actually implementing a defensible protocol.

## Research record
For each substantive source record: stable ID, title, authors, year, DOI/URL, version, publication status, license/access rights, date accessed, question addressed, method, dataset/task, exact passage locator (section/page/table/figure), observations, limitations, contradictory evidence, confidence and reviewer. Distinguish author claims from independently verified observations. Do not invent page numbers or quotes.

## Chapter and example acceptance
- Learning objectives, prerequisite vocabulary, plain-language explanation and precise definition.
- Citations at claim level where feasible; exact source pointers for controversial/quantitative claims.
- Separate fact, interpretation, hypothesis, and analogy; label unsupported claims explicitly.
- Vendor-neutral, versioned comparisons. No fabricated prices, features, benchmark scores or rankings.
- Reproducible examples with tested commands, dependencies, expected output, limitations and license checks.
- Diagrams and animations are original or properly licensed; include alt text, captions, and text equivalents.
- Respect privacy, security, evaluation leakage, memory poisoning and forgetting/deletion limitations.

## Review roles
Research reviewer: source and methodological accuracy.
Engineering reviewer: examples, tests, versions, threat model.
Editorial reviewer: accessible and consistent simple English.
Publication owner: confidentiality, licenses, approval to release.

AI-assisted research and drafting must be checked against actual source documents; an LLM summary is not evidence. Feedback and correction history must remain visible.
